---
name: YouTube metadata
description: Title, description, and hashtag guidance for YouTube exports.
---

When writing or proposing YouTube titles, descriptions, or hashtags for an export:

- Keep the title to 100 characters or fewer.
- Write a short description that states what the video is and who it is for.
- Include 3–5 relevant hashtags (not more). Prefer searchable topic tags over filler.

Apply this only when the user is preparing YouTube metadata or a YouTube export. Otherwise ignore it.
